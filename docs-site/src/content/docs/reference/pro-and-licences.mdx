What Pro adds, how the licence works offline, and everything that stays free.

## What stays free, always

Gate, Witness, deeds, Stop, Undo, Insights, `scan`, the scan Action's scan, job summary and SARIF on every repo (and its pull request comment on public repos), and the free policy packs. Nothing free today becomes paid.

## What Pro adds

The scan Action's pull request comment on private repos, `scan --full` (the full report), Pro policy packs and the fix writer (as they ship), one-step session undo, and the security review pack.

## Prices

Pro is $29 a month or $290 a year. The full scan report is $29 once, and counts toward Pro within 30 days. Team and Cloud: join the waitlist. Want a person to look? Reviews from $750, on request.

## Activate a licence

```sh test=skip reason="licences ship in S48"
deedglass licence activate <key>
deedglass licence show
```

The key arrives by email from Dodo, our payment provider and reseller, and on the return page. The CLI activates the key with Dodo, then our server checks it again and returns a signed record, saved to `.deedglass/licence.json` (mode 0600; `DEEDGLASS_LICENCE_FILE` moves it). `deedglass licence refresh` and `deedglass licence deactivate` do what they say.

## Offline and grace

Free features send nothing to deedglass.com: the free scan reads files on your machine and, with `--postgres-dsn-env`, connects only to the database you name. A Pro licence is checked with Dodo Payments and deedglass.com: `deedglass serve` asks at start and then daily, and Pro commands ask when you run them; a request goes out when the last successful check is a day old, and at once when the clock runs more than 5 minutes behind the last check or the latest time seen, or when `licence.clock` is missing, unreadable or altered. `deedglass licence activate`, `refresh` and `deactivate` always call out. `DEEDGLASS_NO_NETWORK=1` stops every licence call, and only those: `deedglass version --check` and the `--latest` option of `deedglass policy install` and `policy packs` contact GitHub when you use them. Head timestamps are off by default; with TSAs or a transparency service listed in deedglass.yaml, `deedglass deeds timestamp` and each export contact only those.

`licence.clock` sits next to `.deedglass/licence.json`. Offline, Pro keeps working for up to **14 days** after the last successful check. After that, Pro is read-only: existing full reports open, installed Pro packs keep enforcing, and `scan --full` refuses. The exact line:

```text
Pro paused: your licence was last checked 15 days ago. Run deedglass licence refresh. Everything free still works.
```

On a private repo with a licence set, the scan Action checks it online on every run: it activates the key with Dodo Payments and deedglass.com, reads it back offline, and frees the activation with Dodo Payments at the end. Without a licence that includes the comment, it adds no new comment and its job summary says why.

## Free features never read the licence

No network call and no telemetry. The free test suite passes with an expired, corrupt or missing licence.

## Billing

Invoices, cancelling and the payment method are behind the link Dodo emails you. Refunds follow the refunds page on deedglass.com.
