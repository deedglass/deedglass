By the end, an action that needs approval posts a card in Slack, and the right person approves it there.

**Before you start**

- A Slack workspace where you can install an app.
- A Deedglass server reachable from Slack over HTTPS (Slack posts button clicks to it).

## 1. Make the Slack app

```sh test title="Terminal"
$ deedglass slack manifest --base-url https://deedglass.example.com
…
```

Paste the manifest into api.slack.com › Your apps › Create from manifest, install it, and keep the bot token and signing secret.

## 2. Connect it

```sh test=skip reason="needs a Slack app's bot token and signing secret"
deedglass connector create --config slack.yaml
deedglass connector credentials set slack --field bot_token=env:SLACK_BOT_TOKEN --field signing_secret=env:SLACK_SIGNING_SECRET
```

## 3. Notify Slack from a rule

Add `notify: [slack]` to a `require_approval` rule. Its approvals post a card to the approvals channel: the agent, the tool, the arguments (redacted), the rule's reason, and Approve and Deny buttons.

## 4. Approve

A click is checked against the Slack signing secret within a 5-minute window, and replays are refused. The person's Slack email must match a confirmed Deedglass account, or the click is refused with `approver_unmapped`. Four-eyes and the self rule apply as in the Console. The card updates as votes arrive, and shows the final verdict.

## What it doesn't do

<Limits>
- Slack is a way to vote, not the record. The deed in Deedglass is the record.
- A Slack account without a matching Deedglass account can't approve.
- Deedglass doesn't read your channels. It posts cards and receives button clicks.
</Limits>
