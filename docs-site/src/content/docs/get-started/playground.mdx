See an agent say 1 row while 301 changed, block it, and undo it, in your browser.

**Before you start**

- A browser. Nothing to install, no account.

## Open it

Go to `deedglass.com/play`. Everything runs in your browser: Postgres (PGlite) and the policy engine (cel-js) are served from deedglass.com, and the page makes no third-party requests.

## The three scenes

1. **A blocked `DROP`.** The agent sends `DROP TABLE public.orders`. The policy denies it before Postgres sees it, and a deed records the attempt.
2. **1 row reported, 301 changed.** The agent updates one order. A trigger on the table changes 300 more. The verdict is `L2 · MISMATCH`, with the diff.
3. **Approve, then Undo.** You countersign the write as a person would, then undo it and watch the table go back.

## Edit the policy

A 6-line policy sits beside the scenes. Change a rule and run the scene again; the decision and the deed change with it. Each deed can be checked in the page.

The playground compares table snapshots in your browser. The real Witness reads Postgres's own change log.

## What it doesn't do

<Limits>
- The playground's policy engine is cel-js running compiled policies. The product compiles and runs them in Go.
- Its verdicts come from comparing snapshots, not from a change log.
- Nothing you do there reaches a server. Close the tab and it's gone.
</Limits>
