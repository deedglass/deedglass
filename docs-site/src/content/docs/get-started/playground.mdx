See an agent say 1 row while 301 changed, block it, and undo it, in your browser.

**Before you start**

- A browser with `DecompressionStream` (Safari 16.4 or later, current Chrome, Edge or Firefox). Nothing to install, no account.

## Open it

Go to `deedglass.com/play`. Everything runs in the tab: Postgres (PGlite) with the demo's Acme Store schema and trigger, and the rules engine (cel-js). Both are served from deedglass.com, and the page makes no third-party requests.

## The three scenes

1. **A blocked `DROP`.** The agent sends `DROP TABLE public.orders`. The rules refuse it before Postgres sees it: `✕ Blocked by policy "block-destructive-sql"`, and all 500 rows stay as they were.
2. **1 row reported, 301 changed.** The agent ships order 42 and reports one row. A trigger on the table ships 300 more. The check says `✕ Doesn't match · said 1 row · changed 301` and names the trigger. Press **Undo** and all 301 rows go back: `✓ 301 rows restored`.
3. **Edit the policy, then approve.** Change the 6-line policy so writes wait for a person and run the scene again. It waits (`◐ Waiting for you to approve`); approve it, then undo it.

## Check the records

Every step is sealed as a record in the tab. **Check these deeds** runs the same offline check as `/verify`. Change one byte and the check fails.

**Share this scene** appears after a scene has run. The link holds the scene and your policy in the part after `#`, which never reaches a server.

## What it doesn't do

<Limits>
- The playground compares table snapshots in your browser. Deedglass itself reads Postgres's own change log.
- Its rules engine is cel-js running rules exported from the Go engine; the two agree on all 40 test cases in the parity suite.
- Nothing you do there reaches a server. Close the tab and it's gone.
</Limits>
