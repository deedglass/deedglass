# The Console design system

The Console (S24) is built from one set of tokens, `web/tokens/tokens.json`, kept in lockstep with
the repo's [`DESIGN.md`](../DESIGN.md) front matter (`internal/console` `TestTokensMatchDesignMD`
fails when a colour, radius, duration or Console type token exists in one and not the other).
`web/scripts/gen-tokens.mjs` writes `web/src/styles/tokens.css`, the token block of
`web/verify/src/ui/styles.css`, the favicon, the contrast table the kit shows, and the tables
below. `DEEDGLASS_BRAND.md` wins any disagreement; the skills are a lens, never a source.

**Rules that hold everywhere**

- About 90% of every screen is neutral. One accent (links, primary buttons, focus) and the verdict
  colours. Aurora appears in exactly two places: the Mirror in the sidebar and the Sealed moment.
- Red means deny, mismatch or destructive (Stop), nothing else.
- Every chip pairs its colour with an icon and a word.
- Machine values (ids, hashes, tool names, SQL) are Geist Mono; people's words are Inter.
- No hex value outside `tokens.json` (a Vitest check), no inline style, no inline script.

The terminal has its own short style guide, the same rules in sixteen colours: symbols with words,
errors that end with a next step, aligned columns, a progress line for slow steps
([COPY_CATALOG.md › CLI style](COPY_CATALOG.md#cli-style-every-command)).

## Contrast

`node web/scripts/contrast.mjs` checks every declared pair in both themes (WCAG 2.2: text 4.5:1,
UI 3:1; hairlines are decorative and listed as such) and exits 1 on any failure. It reproduces the
S24 findings, each solved by a role assignment inside the brand palette (no new colour):

| Brand pair | Ratio | Role used instead |
|---|---|---|
| White on dark accent `#6F8DFF` | 3.03 | Dark primary buttons use `ink-950` text (6.61) |
| White on dark danger `#FF7A70` | 2.54 | Filled red buttons in dark use `ink-950` text (7.88) |
| `slate-500` on `mist-100` | 4.26 | Secondary text on raised light surfaces uses `#3F4453` (8.37), the `text-2-on-alt` role: kbd hints, the segmented control, menus |
| `slate-300` input border on white | 1.88 | Light inputs use `slate-400` (3.18) and sit on `surface`, never `paper-50` (2.97); dark inputs use `slate-500` on `ink-900` (3.85) |

## Type

Inter Variable (optical size axis, `cv11`, `ss01`) for everything, Geist Mono 400 for machine values,
both self-hosted (80.8 KB of fonts), with the site's metric-matched fallback so nothing shifts on
load. `tabular-nums` on every table, metric and time. The Console's compact scale is
`console-*` in DESIGN.md (title-2 24/30, title-3 20/26, headline 15/22, body 14/20, body-dense 13/18,
footnote 13/18, caption 12/16 small caps, metric 28/32, mono-s 12.5/18). Verdict chips keep their
capitals (`VERIFIED`) at 12 px with +0.04em tracking; other chips are sentence case.

## Space, shape and layout

4-pt grid (0, 2, 4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, 32, 40, 48, 64). Radii 6 (chips, inputs), 10 (buttons), 14
(cards), 20 (sheets, palette). Sidebar 248 px from 1024 px, a 64 px icon rail from 768, a top bar and
a bottom bar below 768. Content max 1440 px with 24 px gutters (16 below 1024). Rows and controls
36 px (32 small, 44 under `pointer: coarse`). Below 640 px tables become compact cards: the first
cell as the title, the verdict and time on one line, the rest on the detail page.

## Elevation and glass

Light uses two soft shadows (cards, overlays); dark uses surface steps and hairlines, plus one overlay
shadow under glass. Glass (`blur(20px) saturate(140%)`, a 1 px inner top highlight, a hairline) is used
only on the top bar once content scrolls under it, sheets, the palette, menus and toasts; without
`backdrop-filter` (or under `prefers-reduced-transparency`) it is the opaque raised surface.

## Motion and the Sealed moment

120 / 180 / 240 / 400 ms; enter `cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.2,1)`, exit `cubic-bezier(.4,0,1,1)`. No page
transitions. The Mirror (`web/verify/src/ui/mirror.ts`, one implementation for the verify page and
the Console) draws the Split Orb at 16, 20, 32 or 48 px in five states:

| State | What it shows |
|---|---|
| open | the halves apart with the pane between them (the logo) |
| sealed | 0–240 ms the halves meet on the pane; 240–640 ms one teal ring grows r 44 → 58 and fades from 20% |
| mismatch | 0–240 ms the halves stop short; 240–420 ms a 1-unit red hairline fades in. No shake |
| pending | apart, the right half's rim in the pending colour |
| unverifiable | apart, the right half's rim dashed in the unverifiable colour |

It animates with the Web Animations API (no inline styles), plays only on a live transition, once per
deed per page, at most 3 plays a second (`web/src/lib/seal.ts`), and under reduced motion shows the
end state at once with a 180 ms fade. The Mirror is `aria-hidden`; the chip beside it carries the
meaning. History rows never show it.

## Icons

Lucide (ISC) at 1.5 px (stroke 1.8 at 20 px from the 24-unit box), plus four custom icons in the same
style: **Deed** (a folded page with a seal), **Witness** (the claimed half facing a glass line),
**Gate** (an arch) and **Stop** (a rounded square). `web/src/icons/used.json` names every icon that
ships; a test fails on a missing or unused one. Icons are `aria-hidden`; icon-only buttons have an
`aria-label`.

## Components

The kit at `/app/_kit` (dev builds only) shows each one in every state: Button and IconButton;
Input, Textarea, Select, Checkbox, Radio, Switch, SegmentedControl and Field; VerdictChip,
DecisionChip, StateChip (the 15 lifecycle states), StatusChip and LevelBadge; Badge; IdChip and
HashChip; RelativeTime; Table; Card, Section, KeyValue and Tabs; Sheet, Dialog and ConfirmDialog
(typed); Popover, Menu and Tooltip; Toast and Banner; EmptyState, ErrorState, PermissionState and
Skeleton; CodeBlock, Timeline, Sparkline, Meter and Kbd; the Mirror; the live region.

**Do:** one primary button per view; verbs on buttons (`Stop all agents`, never `Confirm`); empty
states that teach in one sentence with one action; errors that end with a next step and show the
request id.

**Don't:** aurora anywhere but the Mirror; red for anything but deny, mismatch or destructive; a
filled Denied chip (it is an outline so it never reads as a MISMATCH); spinners (skeletons and `◐`
instead); exclamation marks, emoji or em dashes in copy.

## Accessibility checklist

- WCAG 2.2 AA contrast by script; the focus ring is 2 px accent with a 2 px offset on every
  focusable element; the sticky top bar never covers a focused element (`scroll-padding-top`).
- Full keyboard: a skip link, every destination reachable by Tab, `⌘K`/`Ctrl K` and `/` for the
  palette, `g` sequences to go, `j`/`k` in lists, `?` for the list of shortcuts. No shortcut changes
  anything on its own.
- Sheets and dialogs trap focus, close on Escape and a click outside, return focus to the opener,
  and make the page behind them inert.
- Live feeds announce through `aria-live="polite"`; errors through `role="alert"`.
- Targets 36 px (44 on touch); reduced motion and reduced transparency respected; `prefers-contrast:
  more` strengthens hairlines and secondary text.
- Gates: axe 0 serious or critical on the kit, the shell and `/login` in both themes; Lighthouse
  accessibility and best practices ≥ 95 (100 measured).
